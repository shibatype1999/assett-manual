# AI Features (Apple Intelligence)
<!-- position: 11 -->
<!-- description: Suggested settings for assets, adding several assets at once, auto-organizing categories, this month's review, and goal advice with Apple Intelligence. -->

Asset Manager can use Apple Intelligence to help you register and organize assets, and to write short reviews and advice.

Apple Intelligence **runs only on your device**. Data such as your assets and balances is never sent anywhere.

## Supported devices

- Available on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, with iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on.
- All of the features below also work on other devices. In that case, the app creates the content from its own totals and rules instead (the texts follow fixed patterns).

You can check whether it's available under **Settings** → **AI features (Apple Intelligence)**.

## Turning AI features on or off

Use **Use Apple Intelligence** under **Settings** → **AI features (Apple Intelligence)**.
When it's off, the app doesn't use Apple Intelligence and creates content from its own totals and rules only.

## Suggested settings for assets

When you add a new asset and type its name, an icon, currency, and category that match the name may appear as **Suggested settings**.

- Tap **Use these settings** to apply them all at once.
- Tap **Don't use** to close the suggestion.

## Adding several assets at once

Describe your accounts and assets in your own words to register several assets at once.

1. On the **Asset list** screen (pencil icon at the top right of the **Input** tab, or **Settings** → **Asset list**), tap the sparkle icon (**Add several at once**) at the top right.
2. Describe your accounts and assets.
   e.g. Chase checking, Fidelity 401k, Vanguard index fund, Bitcoin, gold savings
3. Tap **Read**.
4. Check the list of assets to be created (name, icon, currency, and category). Uncheck any you don't need.
5. Tap **Add …**.

If nothing can be read, try separating the assets with commas.

## Auto-organizing categories

The app suggests a category for each asset that isn't in one, and you save them all at once after checking.

1. Under **Settings** → **Categories**, tap the sparkle icon (**Auto-organize categories**) at the top right.
2. A category is shown for each asset. If no category fits, a new category (marked **New**) may be suggested.
3. Tap an asset to change where it goes. Choose **Don't assign** to leave it out of categories.
4. Tap **Apply**.

## This month's review

Add the **This month's review** panel to the dashboard to see a short text about how your assets moved this month (→ [Dashboard](dashboard)).

- The change in total assets since the end of last month
- Assets that went up or down the most (for foreign currencies and the like, including how much came from exchange rates and prices)
- Changes in exchange rates and in the share of deposits and cash
- The number of assets you haven't recorded yet this month

## Goal advice

Add the **Goal advice** panel to the dashboard to see your progress toward your asset goal and some guidelines.

- Progress toward the goal and the remaining amount
- When you can expect to reach it at your current pace
- Your average monthly increase over the past year
- If you set **Target year** under **Settings** → **Asset Goal**, the monthly amount needed to reach the goal by that year
- A caution if a large share of your assets is volatile (such as stocks, crypto, or gold)

## About the review and advice texts

- All numbers are calculated by the app from your records. Apple Intelligence only puts those numbers into words.
- Below each panel, "Created on this device (your data never leaves it)" or "Created from the app's totals" shows how the text was created.
- The same text is shown as long as your records don't change. Tap **Create again** on the panel to write it again.
- The advice is an estimate based on your records, not investment advice.

> **Caution**
> Suggestions and texts created by AI features may contain errors. Check them before saving or relying on them.

# Viewing Trends in Charts
<!-- position: 6 -->
<!-- description: How to view trends of your total assets, assets, and categories, and what caused the changes. -->

The **Chart** tab shows how your total assets or the balance of each asset or category has changed over time, in charts and tables.

![Chart tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/chart-line.png)

All amounts in the charts are converted to your default currency. Scroll the whole screen up and down to see everything.

## Choosing what to show and the period

Swipe left or right inside the **Asset** and **Period** frames at the top. The item that stops in the **center frame** is selected. You can also tap a visible item to select it.

- **Asset** – Choose **All** for your total assets, or an asset name for that asset only.
- **Period** – Choose **1M**, **3M**, **6M**, **1Y**, **3 years**, **5 years**, **All**, or **Custom**.

With **Custom**, set the start and end dates with the scroll wheels. The selected range is shown above the chart; tap it to change the range.

> **Notes**
> - If you use categories, switch with **By asset** / **By category** at the top. With **By category**, the **Asset** frame becomes **Category** and trends are shown per category (→ [Grouping with Categories](categories)).
> - **All** and **Custom** are Premium features (marked with a lock icon).

## Switching chart types

Use the buttons below the chart to switch chart types.

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Line Chart | Shows the trend as a single line. When **All** is selected and you have set an asset goal, a red line at the goal amount is also shown. |
| Stacked Chart | Stacks the amount of each asset (or category) as an **Asset Trend**. Liabilities are shown below zero. |
| Bar Chart | Shows the amount of each asset (or category) as stacked bars. |
| Change factors | Groups changes in balance by the subcategories attached to records (see below). |

- **Stacked Chart** and **Bar Chart** are available only when **All** is selected.
- Tap the chart to see the amount at that point.
- In **Bar Chart** and **Change factors**, tap a legend item to show or hide it in the chart. If there are many legend items, only the top ones are shown and the rest open with the + button.

![Stacked chart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/chart-stacked.png)

## Viewing change factors

**Change factors** takes each record's change from the previous record and groups it by that record's subcategory. For example, you can separate increases from your salary from increases due to changes in valuation.

- **Bar chart (top)** – Splits the period by month (or by day for periods of one month or less) and stacks the changes by subcategory, color-coded. Increases are shown above zero and decreases below.
- **List (Change factors in period)** – Totals the changes in the period by subcategory, largest first.

Records without a subcategory are counted as **Uncategorized**. Each asset's first record has no previous record to compare with, so it is not counted.

![Change factors](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/chart-change-factors.png)

## Trend table

Except in **Change factors**, a table of date, amount, and change is shown below the chart.

- Switch the grouping with **Day**, **Month**, or **Year**. For **Month** and **Year**, the last amount in each period is shown.
- **Change** is the difference from the previous period. Increases are shown in green and decreases in red.
- Tap a column header to sort by that column. Tap it again to switch between ascending and descending order.
- Use **Per page** to choose how many rows to show per page (30 / 50 / 100). If there are more rows, use the page numbers below the table.

![Trend table](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/chart-table.png)

> **If no chart appears**
> If the selected period doesn't contain enough records to draw a chart (at least two different dates), "No records to display for this period" is shown.
> Choose a longer period, or add a record on another date.

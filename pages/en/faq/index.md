## Using the app

### Q. Is this an app for recording income and expenses?

No. This app records the **balances** of your assets.
By recording the balances of your bank and brokerage accounts regularly, for example once a month, you can see how your assets change over time.
→ [Getting Started](getting-started)

### Q. What is the difference between an asset and a category?

An "asset" is the unit you record balances for (e.g. Bank A, Broker B), and a "category" is a group of similar assets (e.g. Deposits & Cash, Stocks & Funds).
If you put assets into categories, you can show charts and asset composition as totals per category.
→ [Grouping with Categories](categories)

### Q. Can I track loans or credit card balances?

Yes. Turn on **Treat as Liability** for an asset, and its amounts are counted as negative and subtracted from your total assets. The **Assets and liabilities** panel on the dashboard shows your net worth.
→ [Managing Assets](assets)

### Q. Can I track foreign currency deposits or foreign stocks?

Yes. Set the asset's currency to, for example, US Dollar. You can record amounts in that currency, and your total assets are converted to your default currency.
→ [Currencies and Exchange Rates](currency)

### Q. Can I record gold or crypto?

Yes. You can choose Gold (g / ozt) as a currency. Bitcoin and Ethereum are Premium features. Record the quantity, and it is converted using the latest market price.

### Q. Can I include points or miles in my assets?

Yes. Choose **Points** or **Miles** as the asset's currency. They are initially converted as 1 pt = 1 JPY and 1 mi = 1 JPY. You can change the rate under **Settings** → **Currency & Rates** → **Custom Currency Rates**.

### Q. Can I record why a balance changed?

If you attach subcategories such as **Salary** or **Valuation** to your records, the **Change factors** view in the chart totals what caused the changes (Premium feature).
→ [Viewing Trends in Charts](chart)

### Q. I entered a record incorrectly.

Tap the asset to open its record list, then tap the incorrect record to edit it.
→ [Recording Balances](recording)

## Display

### Q. The chart says "No records to display for this period".

To show a chart, the selected period needs records on at least two different dates. Choose a longer period, or add a record on another date.

### Q. The "By asset" / "By category" buttons don't appear.

These buttons appear only when at least one category contains assets. Put assets into a category under **Settings** → **Categories**.

### Q. "Some currency rates could not be fetched and are not included in the total" appears.

An asset uses a currency whose rate is unknown. Go to **Settings** → **Currency & Rates** and fetch the rate for that currency, or enter it manually.
→ [Currencies and Exchange Rates](currency)

### Q. I can't refresh exchange rates.

Rates can be fetched up to 2 times per day. If you have reached the limit, please try again the next day.
Also check that you are connected to the internet.

### Q. Some assets don't appear in the pie chart or Asset Composition.

The pie chart, treemap, and the Asset Composition table only show assets with a positive amount. Liability assets and assets with a balance of zero are not shown.

### Q. I want to change how numbers are formatted (separators, decimal point).

You can change the decimal places, rounding, and number format under **Settings** → **Display Format**.
→ [Settings](settings)

## Premium

### Q. Can I use the app for free?

Yes. The basic features, such as up to 10 assets, the line chart, periods up to 1 year, and local backup, are free.
→ [Premium](premium)

### Q. What happens to my data if I cancel Premium?

Your recorded data is kept. Assets you registered beyond the free limit (10 assets) can still be used, but you can't add new ones.

## Data

### Q. Where is my data stored?

All data is stored on your device. It is not stored on any external server.
(The app accesses rate information on the internet to fetch exchange rates.)

### Q. Do I need an account?

No.

### Q. How do I move my data to a new device? (iOS)

1. On your old device, tap **Settings** → **Backup** → **Export Data**, then use **Save to Files** to save the file to iCloud Drive.
2. Install the app on your new device.
3. On your new device, tap **Settings** → **Backup** → **Import Data**, choose the saved file, and restore it.
4. If you subscribe to Premium, tap **Settings** → **Upgrade** → **Restore purchases**.

The default currency, display format, date format, theme, and dashboard panel layout are not included in backups, so set them up again as needed.
Export and import are Premium features.
→ [Backing Up and Restoring Data](backup)

### Q. What happens to my data if I delete the app?

Deleting the app deletes all recorded data and local backups (including auto backups).
Before deleting the app, save your data as a file with **Export Data**.

### Q. I accidentally deleted an asset or record.

Deleted data cannot be restored. If you have a backup made before the deletion, you can restore from it (restoring overwrites your current data).
→ [Backing Up and Restoring Data](backup)

# Asset Manager User Guide
<!-- position: 2 -->
<!-- description: User guide for the Asset Manager app. -->

Asset Manager lets you record the balances of all your assets (bank deposits, stocks, real estate, crypto, points, and more) in one place and track your total assets over time with charts.

![Home screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/home.png)

## What you can do

- **See your total assets at a glance** – The latest balance of each category is added up and shown as your total assets.
- **Use multiple currencies** – Foreign currencies, gold, Bitcoin, and more are converted with automatically fetched rates and included in the total.
- **Track trends with charts** – View changes with line, stacked, and bar charts.
- **Look back at past composition** – Pick a date to see how your assets were distributed at that time.
- **Set a goal** – Set an asset goal to see your progress.
- **Back up your data** – Save a copy inside the app, or export it as a file to iCloud Drive and other services.

All of your data is stored on your device. No account is required.

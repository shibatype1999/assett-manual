# Viewing Asset Composition

The **Asset Composition** tab shows how much you had in each category on a date you choose.

![Asset Composition tab](../images/en/asset-composition.png)

## How to use

1. Open the **Asset Composition** tab. The composition as of today is shown first.
2. Choose the year, month, and day with the scroll wheels in the middle of the screen.
3. The composition on the selected date is shown as a pie chart and a table.

## What is shown

- **Pie chart** – Each category's share.
- **Category Breakdown table** – Each category's amount and percentage, plus the total (total assets).

Each category's amount is based on its latest record on or before the selected date, converted to your default currency.

> **Notes**
> - Categories with an amount of zero or less (such as liabilities) are not shown in the pie chart or table.
> - If there are no records on or before the selected date, "No records to display for this period" is shown.
> - You can choose years from the year of your oldest record up to the current year.

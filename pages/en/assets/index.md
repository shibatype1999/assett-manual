# Managing Assets
<!-- position: 4 -->
<!-- description: How to add, edit, reorder, and delete assets, and how liability assets work. -->

An "asset" is the unit you record balances for (e.g. Bank A, Broker B, Cash, Mortgage). Register one for each account or wallet.

Manage assets on the **Asset list** screen. Open it with the pencil icon at the top right of the **Input** tab, or from **Settings** → **Asset list**.

![Asset list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/asset-list.png)

## Adding an asset

1. Tap **Add asset** at the bottom right of the **Input** tab or the **Asset list** screen.
2. On the **Add Asset** screen, set the following:

   | Field | Description |
   | --- | --- |
   | Asset Name | The name of the asset (e.g. Bank A, Broker B, Cash). |
   | Asset Currency | The currency used for this asset's records, chosen with the scroll wheel. Defaults to your default currency. |
   | Category | The category to put this asset in. Choose **None** to leave it out of any category. |
   | Treat as Liability | When on, all amounts in this asset are counted as negative (liability). |
   | Color | The color used in charts and elsewhere. |
   | Icon | The icon for the asset. |

3. Tap **Save** at the top right.

![Add Asset screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/asset-form.png)

> **Notes**
> - If you choose a currency other than your default currency, you can check and change its rate under **Settings** → **Currency & Rates** (→ [Currencies and Exchange Rates](currency)).
> - Manage loans and unpaid credit card balances in an asset with **Treat as Liability** turned on, so they are subtracted from your total assets.
> - The free version allows up to 10 assets. With Premium, you can add unlimited assets (→ [Premium](premium)).
> - Choosing Bitcoin or Ethereum as the currency is a Premium feature.

## Editing an asset

On the **Asset list** screen, tap the asset you want to edit. Make your changes on the **Edit Asset** screen and tap **Save**.

## Reordering assets

On the **Asset list** screen, press and hold the **≡** handle on the right of an asset, then drag it up or down.
The order is also used on the **Input** tab, in charts, and elsewhere.

## Deleting assets

You can delete several assets at once.

1. Tap the edit button (pencil icon) at the top right of the **Asset list** screen.
2. Check the boxes to the left of the assets you want to delete.
3. Tap the trash icon at the bottom left.
4. Tap **Delete** in the confirmation dialog.
5. Tap the done button (✓) at the top right to leave edit mode.

> **Caution**
> Deleting an asset also **deletes all of its records**, and this cannot be undone.
> Back up your data first if needed (→ [Backing Up and Restoring Data](backup)).

## About the default assets

When you first launch the app, three assets are provided: **Bank**, **Stocks**, and **Real Estate**. They are placed in the **Deposits & Cash**, **Stocks & Funds**, and **Real Estate** categories.
You can freely edit or delete them. If you haven't renamed them, their names change to match the app's display language when you switch languages.

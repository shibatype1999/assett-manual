# Privacy Policy

[日本語版](../privacy-policy)

The operator (the "Operator") of the Asset Manager app (the "App") sets out this privacy policy (the "Policy") describing how user information is handled.

This Policy is a translation of the Japanese version. If there is any discrepancy between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

## 1. Basic policy

The App is designed so that the data you enter is managed only on your device. The Operator does not collect data such as your assets or balances.

## 2. Data entered in the App

- Data you register in the App, such as assets, balances, records, memos, goals, and settings, is stored only on your device.
- This data is not sent to the Operator's servers or anywhere else.
- No account is required to use the App, and the App does not ask for personal information such as your name or email address.
- Deleting the App deletes the data stored on your device (including backups inside the App).

## 3. Communication with external services

To obtain exchange rates and gold and crypto prices, the App communicates with the following external services.

| Service | Information obtained |
| --- | --- |
| Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.app) | Exchange rates |
| CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) | Crypto prices |
| xaus.com | Gold prices |

- The App sends only the currencies whose rates it needs. It does not send data such as your assets or balances.
- When communicating, information normally included in internet communication, such as your device's IP address, may be sent to these services. Such information is handled according to each service's own policies.

## 4. Purchasing paid services (Premium)

- Subscriptions for the App are purchased and paid for through Apple Inc.'s App Store.
- The Operator does not obtain payment information such as your credit card details.
- Your purchase status is checked on your device using the App Store's mechanisms. Purchase information is handled according to Apple Inc.'s privacy policy.

## 5. Backup files

Files created with **Export Data** in the App are saved to the destination you choose (services such as iCloud Drive, or other apps). They are handled there according to each service's own policies.

## 6. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App does not use analytics tools, advertising, or tracking to follow users.

## 7. Disclosure to third parties

Because the Operator does not collect data such as your assets or balances, it does not provide such data to third parties.

## 8. Information received through inquiries

Your name, email address, type and content of inquiry, and device and app version sent through the contact form are used only to respond to your inquiry and to improve the App, and are not provided to third parties except as required by law.

To prevent spam, the sender's IP address is converted into a form that cannot be restored and kept for up to one hour to check the number of submissions.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

The Operator may change this Policy as necessary. When doing so, the Operator will set an effective date and, before that date, announce the revised Policy and the effective date on this page.

## 10. Contact

For inquiries about this Policy, please use our [contact form](../contact/en).

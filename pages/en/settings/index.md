## Asset Goal

Set a target amount for your total assets.

1. Tap **Asset Goal**.
2. Choose a **Currency** and enter the **Goal Amount**.
3. If you like, choose a **Target year** (from this year up to 40 years ahead, or **Not set**). When set, the **Goal advice** panel on the dashboard shows a guideline for the monthly amount needed to reach the goal by that year.
4. Tap **Save**.

If you enter the goal in a currency other than your default currency, it is converted to your default currency and saved.

Turn on **Show goal on charts** to draw a red line at your goal amount on the total asset charts (on by default). This switch is saved as soon as you change it.

Once a goal is set, the achievement rate appears on the **Total Assets** panel of the dashboard. Add the **Asset Goal** panel to also see the remaining amount and estimated achievement date (→ [Dashboard](dashboard)).

## Display Format

Set how amounts are displayed.

- **Decimal Places** – Choose **None**, **1**, or **2**.
- **Rounding** – Choose how hidden digits are handled: **Floor**, **Round**, or **Ceiling**.
- **Number format** – Choose the thousands separator and decimal point.

  | Format | Example |
  | --- | --- |
  | Comma separators | 1,234,567.89 |
  | No separators | 1234567.89 |
  | Period separators (comma decimal) | 1.234.567,89 |
  | Space separators (comma decimal) | 1 234 567,89 |
  | Apostrophe separators | 1'234'567.89 |

This only changes how amounts are displayed; the recorded amounts themselves don't change.
In amount input fields, always enter numbers like "1,234.56", whichever format you choose.

## Date Format

Choose how all dates in the app are shown. The screen shows today's date in the selected format as an example.

Available formats (for August 19, 2026): 2026/08/19, 19/8/26, 8/19/26, 8/19/2026, 19/08/2026, 19.08.2026, 19-08-2026, 2026/8/19, 2026.08.19, 2026-08-19

## Skin

A skin changes the design of the whole app (colors, corner shapes, and fonts) all at once. **Silk Satin** is selected at first.
Each skin shows a preview (title area, total assets, and bottom menu). Tap one to switch to it right away.

| Group | Skins |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | Standard, Cool, Cute, Hot, Ice |
| Luxury | Black Card, Midnight Dial, Quilted Leather, Crocodile, Gold Bar, Diamond, Silk Satin, Royal Gold, Green Dial, Emerald Croco, Emerald Cut, Jade, Matcha & Gold, Sage Silk |

**Theme** and **Theme Color** can be used only with the **Standard** skin. With other skins, they show **Standard only**.

Choose the app's main color (Standard skin only). When you pick a color, the **Preview** shows right away how the total assets panel, buttons, and bottom menu will look.

## Passcode Lock

A passcode is required when you open the app and when you return to it from another app.

1. Tap **Settings** → **Passcode Lock**.
2. Turn on **Passcode Lock**.
3. Choose the number of digits (**4 digits** or **6 digits**) and enter a new passcode.
4. Enter the same passcode again to confirm.

- To change the passcode, tap **Change passcode**. To stop using it, turn off **Passcode Lock**. Both require the current passcode.
- After repeated failed attempts, you have to wait a while before trying again.

> **Caution**
> If you forget the passcode, you will not be able to open the app, and there is no way to recover it. We recommend exporting a backup before setting one (→ [Backing Up and Restoring Data](backup)).

> **Tip**
> To lock the app with Face ID, we recommend long-pressing this app's icon on the Home Screen and choosing **Require Face ID** (iOS 18 or later).

## Subcategories

Manage the subcategories (e.g. Salary, Valuation) that you attach to records.

- **Add** – Tap **+** at the bottom right, enter a name, and tap **Save**.
- **Rename** – Tap a subcategory, change the name, and tap **Save**.
- **Reorder** – Press and hold the **≡** handle on the right, then drag it up or down.
- **Delete** – Tap the edit button (pencil icon) at the top right, tap the trash icon, then tap **Delete** in the confirmation dialog.

Deleting a subcategory does not delete any records (the subcategory label simply disappears from those records).

## Language

Choose the app's display language. Choose **Follow system setting** to match your device's language setting.

Available languages: 日本語, English, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Deutsch, Français, Русский, Bahasa Indonesia, Português, العربية, ไทย, Italiano

If you haven't renamed the default assets and subcategories, their names change to match the selected language. The names of default categories are also shown in the selected language.

# Asset Manager User Guide
<!-- position: 2 -->
<!-- description: User guide for the Asset Manager app. -->

Asset Manager lets you record the balances of all your assets (bank deposits, stocks, real estate, crypto, points, and more) in one place and track your total assets over time with charts.

![Dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/dashboard.png)

## What you can do

- **See your total assets at a glance** – Arrange panels such as total assets, asset breakdown, and month-over-month change on the dashboard.
- **Record balances for each asset** – Register accounts such as bank or brokerage accounts as "assets" and record their balances by date. Besides quick entry one at a time, you can record several at once with batch entry or record from a calendar.
- **Move from other apps** – Import assets and balances in bulk from screenshots of banking or brokerage apps, or from CSV files from Excel and other apps.
- **Group assets into categories** – Put similar assets into a "category" (e.g. Deposits & Cash, Stocks & Funds) and view charts by category.
- **Use multiple currencies** – Foreign currencies, gold, crypto, and more are converted with automatically fetched rates and included in the total.
- **Track trends with charts** – View changes with line, stacked, and bar charts, plus a change-factors chart.
- **Look back at past composition** – Pick a date to see how your assets were distributed, as a pie chart or treemap.
- **Set a goal** – Set an asset goal to see your progress and estimated achievement date.
- **Get help from AI** – With Apple Intelligence, add several assets at once, organize categories, and get this month's review and goal advice (it runs only on your device).
- **Back up your data** – Save a copy inside the app, set up automatic backups, or export a file to iCloud Drive and other services. You can also encrypt it with a password.
- **Make it your own** – Choose from 29 skins, including luxury and gem designs, and lock the app with a passcode.

All of your data is stored on your device. No account is required.

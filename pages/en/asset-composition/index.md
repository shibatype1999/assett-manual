# Viewing Asset Composition
<!-- position: 7 -->
<!-- description: How to view your asset composition on a given date as a pie chart or treemap. -->

The **Asset Composition** tab shows how much you had in each asset (or category) on a date you choose.

![Asset Composition tab](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/asset-composition.png)

## How to use

1. Open the **Asset Composition** tab. The composition as of today is shown first.
2. Choose the year, month, and day with the scroll wheels. You can choose from the date of your first record up to today.
3. The composition on the selected date is shown.

## What is shown

- **Total Assets** – Your total assets on the selected date and the change from one month earlier.
- **Pie chart / Treemap** – Switch with the **Pie chart** and **Treemap** buttons. The treemap shows each asset as a rectangle sized in proportion to its amount.
- **Asset Breakdown table** – Each asset's amount and percentage, plus the total (total assets).

Each asset's amount is based on its latest record on or before the selected date, converted to your default currency.

If you use categories, switch to totals per category with the **By asset** / **By category** buttons. This choice is saved separately from the chart (→ [Grouping with Categories](categories)).

![Treemap](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/assett-manual/main/images/en/asset-composition-treemap.png)

> **Notes**
> - Assets with an amount of zero or less (such as liabilities) are not shown in the chart or table.
> - The legend above the chart is ordered by share, largest first. If there are many items, the top 5 are shown and the rest open with a button.
> - Small sections don't show a name or percentage so that labels don't overlap. Check the legend instead.

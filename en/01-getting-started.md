# Getting Started

## Screen layout

Use the tabs at the bottom of the screen to switch between five screens.

![Tab bar](../images/en/tab-bar.png)

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Input | Check your total assets and each category's balance, and record balances. |
| Chart | View trends of your total assets or each category in charts and tables. |
| Asset Composition | See how your assets were distributed on a given date. |
| Category | Add, edit, reorder, and delete categories. |
| Settings | Set your asset goal, currencies, backups, display options, and more. |

## The basic idea

This app records **balances as of a given date**, not income and expenses.

- Create a **category** for each type of asset (e.g. Bank, Stocks, Real Estate).
- Record the **balance** of each category on a given day.
- The **latest record** of each category becomes its current balance, and the sum of all categories is your **total assets**.

For example, if you record a bank balance of $10,000 on April 1 and $12,000 on May 1, the current bank balance is $12,000.
The chart shows the change from $10,000 to $12,000.

## First steps

When you first launch the app, three categories are already set up: **Bank**, **Stocks**, and **Real Estate**.

1. **Set up your categories**
   On the **Category** tab, add the categories you need and delete the ones you don't.
   → [Managing Categories](03-categories.md)
2. **Record your balances**
   On the **Input** tab, tap a category and record its current balance.
   → [Recording Balances](02-recording.md)
3. **Set a goal (optional)**
   Enter a goal amount under **Asset Goal** on the **Settings** tab to see your progress.
   → [Settings](08-settings.md)
4. **Record regularly**
   Recording balances on a regular schedule, such as once a month, lets you see trends in the charts.
   → [Viewing Trends in Charts](04-chart.md)

> **Tip**
> To avoid losing important data, we recommend backing up from time to time.
> → [Backing Up and Restoring Data](07-backup.md)
